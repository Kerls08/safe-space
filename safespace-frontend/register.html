Terms of Use

SafeSpace University Sanctuary Agreement • Effective 2026

1. Purpose & Voluntary Engagement

SafeSpace is an institution-affiliated psychological wellness sanctuary developed to provide students and campus mental health professionals with safe, confidential emotional support, psychoeducational tools, peer venting boards, and guided counseling. All registration and participation in SafeSpace is completely voluntary.

2. User Accounts & Verification

Users must provide accurate institutional identification for credential verification. Accounts are personal and non-transferable. You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your chosen password.

3. Community Standards & Safe Conduct

SafeSpace is an empathetic, healing space. By using the platform, you agree to treat every peer and professional with dignity. The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

  • Cyberbullying, harassment, intimidation, or targeted hate speech against any student, faculty, or staff member.
  • Publishing non-consensual personal information, doxxing, or defamatory statements.
  • Intentional misuse or spamming of anonymous rant boards or counseling queue systems.

Violations will result in immediate content removal and permanent revocation of platform privileges.

4. Boundaries of Guidance Counseling & Emergency Situations

Guidance counselors and psychometricians provide psychological first aid, emotional guidance, and supportive triage. SafeSpace is not an emergency healthcare facility or 24/7 psychiatric emergency ward.

Life-Threatening Emergency Notice: If you or someone you know is experiencing severe immediate distress or life-threatening harm, please contact national emergency services immediately:
National Crisis Hotline: 1553 (Toll-Free) or 0966-351-4518.

1. Philippine Data Privacy Act Compliance

SafeSpace strictly adheres to the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Your personal data is collected solely for institutional verification, session routing, and platform integrity, and is never sold, harvested, or shared with unauthorized third parties.

2. Shielded Identity & Decoupled Anonymity

When participating in public or community features (such as the Rant Board), your real name, institutional ID, and personal identifiers are completely decoupled and replaced with randomized anonymous handles. Your peers cannot see your real identity.

3. Limits of Confidentiality & Duty of Care

Counseling confidentiality is sacred. However, in compliance with Philippine mental health laws and professional ethical standards, confidentiality is subject to mandatory ethical limits:

  • When a client poses a clear, immediate, and imminent danger of suicide or severe self-harm.
  • When there is an active, imminent threat to the life or physical safety of another individual.
  • When disclosure is legally mandated by child protection laws or judicial court orders.

In such rare life-safety emergencies, authorized professionals may initiate designated campus safety protocols to protect human life.

4. Data Encryption & Security Standards

Passwords are salted and cryptographically hashed before storage. Data transmission is secured using industry-standard TLS encryption, and access permissions are governed by strict Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC).
